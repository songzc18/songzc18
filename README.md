# Hi, I'm Zichen Song 👋

PhD Candidate in **Computational Neuroscience** at **Kyoto University**  
Visiting Researcher at the **Laboratory of Neural Computation and Optimization (LNCO), EPFL**

My research combines **human EEG**, **computational neuroscience**, and **statistical modeling** to understand how internal cognitive states are represented in the brain and how they influence cognition and adaptive behavior.

## 📖 Current Research

### Neural representation of internal cognitive states

Investigating how anticipated difficulty, prospective confidence, and retrospective confidence are encoded in large-scale human brain activity using EEG and computational analysis.

### Perception-to-imagery neural decoding

Studying whether neural representations established during perception can generalize to mental imagery through machine learning and multivariate EEG decoding.

### Multimodal physiological analysis

Developing computational methods for integrating EEG, ECG, and behavioral data to investigate cognitive and physiological processes.
